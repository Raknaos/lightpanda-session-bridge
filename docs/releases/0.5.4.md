# v0.5.4 - the popup fits, and the buttons are where they belong

## The bug you saw

The button existed, but it was below the fold. Chrome caps a popup at **600px**
tall; the body was pinned to `max-height: 580px` with `overflow: hidden`, and the
real content needed about 590px. The update card landed past the cap and its
button row was sliced in half - unclickable, and it looked broken.

The body now scrolls instead of hiding, the vertical density was trimmed, and the
whole popup fits in **563px** in its default state, footer included. Measured, not
guessed: `scripts/diagnostics/preview_popup.py` reports 563px and fails the build
if a future change pushes it back over the cap.

## What else moved

- **"Tout retirer" is no longer a full-width red bar on its own row.** It is a
  compact pill in the sessions header, next to the count badge, so it reads as a
  secondary action instead of a primary one that got lost.
- **The commit hash is printed once.** The blue chip said `a478b90` and the line
  under it said "new commit a478b90". The chip now says the state (`Update` /
  `Up to date`) and the line says what you would get (`-> commit a478b90`).
- **The clear button keeps its label.** The click handler wrote `textContent` on
  the button itself, replacing its markup and detaching the label node on the
  first click.
- **The rollback action is a 38px icon button** - two long French labels cannot
  share one 430px row. The sentence lives in the tooltip and `aria-label`.
- The collapsible sessions card moved to the end, so opening the list only grows
  the tail, and the list scrolls inside a capped area instead of pushing the
  layout around.
